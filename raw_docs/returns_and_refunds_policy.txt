The returns and refunds policy outlines the conditions under which customers
can return purchased products and receive refunds, replacements, or store
credits. These policies are designed to balance customer satisfaction with
operational feasibility.

Most products are eligible for return within the specified return window,
provided they are unused, undamaged, and returned in their original packaging
with all included accessories and documentation.

Refunds are typically processed after the returned item undergoes inspection
and approval. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, and
processing times may vary depending on payment provider and product category.

Certain product categories may have specific return restrictions. Items such
as hygiene products, personalized goods, consumables, or perishable items may
be non-returnable unless defective or damaged upon delivery.

Customers are encouraged to review category-specific return guidelines or
contact customer support for clarification before initiating a return request.
