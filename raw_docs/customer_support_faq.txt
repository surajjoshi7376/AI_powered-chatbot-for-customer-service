What is customer support?
Customer support is the service provided to help customers resolve questions,
issues, and concerns related to products, orders, payments, and post-purchase
services.

How can I check my order status?
Customers can check order status using tracking details shared after purchase
or by contacting customer support with their order reference number.

How do refunds work?
Refunds are initiated after returned items are received, inspected, and
approved. Once approved, refunds are issued to the original payment method
within the standard processing timeframe.

What should I do if a product is out of stock?
If a product is unavailable, customer support can provide restock estimates
or suggest alternative items from the same or similar categories.

Who should I contact for delivery issues?
Customers experiencing delivery delays, missing items, or damaged shipments
should contact customer support for investigation and resolution.
