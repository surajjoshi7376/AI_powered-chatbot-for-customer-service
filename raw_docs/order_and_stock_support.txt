Order and stock support focuses on assisting customers with order processing,
tracking, and product availability. This includes providing accurate updates
from the time an order is placed until it is successfully delivered.

Customers may contact support to:
- Confirm whether an order has been successfully placed
- Track the current status of an order or shipment
- Understand delivery timelines and delays
- Check availability of products across supported categories
- Request estimated restock timelines for unavailable items

If a product is temporarily out of stock, customer support may suggest similar
or alternative products from the same or related categories, ensuring customers
have suitable replacement options.

Stock availability and delivery estimates may vary based on demand, supplier
constraints, warehouse location, and the specific product category involved.
Support teams rely on system data to provide the most up-to-date information.
