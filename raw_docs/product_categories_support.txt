Customer support provides assistance across a diverse range of product
categories, ensuring that customers receive accurate and relevant guidance
based on the type of product purchased.

Supported categories include, but are not limited to:
- Electronics and accessories
- Home appliances and furniture
- Fashion, footwear, and accessories
- Toys and baby products
- Automotive and tools
- Health, wellness, and personal care
- Sports, fitness, and outdoor equipment
- Home improvement and utility products

Support services may include product information, compatibility checks, usage
guidance, troubleshooting assistance, warranty details, and replacement or
repair eligibility.

Different categories may follow different operational rules. For example,
electronics often involve technical troubleshooting, while fashion products
commonly involve size, fit, or material-related inquiries.
