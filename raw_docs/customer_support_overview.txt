Customer support refers to the structured assistance provided to customers
before, during, and after the purchase of products or services. Its primary
objective is to ensure customer satisfaction by resolving issues efficiently,
providing accurate information, and maintaining consistent communication.

Customer support covers a wide range of interactions, including answering
product-related questions, assisting with order placement, tracking shipments,
handling delivery issues, managing returns and refunds, and addressing post-
purchase concerns.

Support operations are designed to serve customers across multiple product
categories, ensuring that policies, procedures, and responses remain consistent
while still accommodating category-specific requirements.

A well-defined customer support system improves trust, reduces friction in the
customer journey, and helps organizations maintain long-term customer
relationships by delivering reliable and timely assistance.
