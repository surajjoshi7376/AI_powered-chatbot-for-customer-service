DELIVERY_ESTIMATES:
Delivery timelines depend on product category, shipping method, warehouse location, and address.

NO_EXACT_DATES:
Exact delivery dates cannot be guaranteed without order verification.

ADDRESS_CHANGE:
Delivery address changes are allowed only before dispatch.
Once dispatched, address changes may not be possible.

DELIVERY_ISSUES:
If an order is marked delivered but not received, customer support must investigate with logistics partners.
