REFUND_PROCESS:
Refunds are issued only after returned items are received, inspected, and approved.
Refunds are sent to the original payment method.

REFUND_TIMELINES:
Refund timelines depend on payment provider, product category, and bank processing.
Exact dates cannot be guaranteed.

NON_RETURNABLE:
Personalized items are non-returnable unless defective or damaged.
Hygiene products, consumables, and perishables are also non-returnable.
